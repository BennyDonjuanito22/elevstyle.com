Frequently asked

How does payment work?

Single and Bundle orders go through Shopify Pay (the same secure checkout used by 1M+ merchants). The Curated Box subscription goes through Stripe so you can pause or cancel anytime from your account. Major credit and debit cards accepted on both.

Is anything a subscription?

Only the Curated Box tier — it's a $97 quarterly drop you can pause or cancel anytime. Single and Bundle orders are one-time purchases with no recurring charge.

What about sizing and returns?

Each product page has a fit guide. If a piece doesn't fit, you have 30 days from delivery to return it (unworn, unwashed, tags attached). Email elevere@asiinnovativesolutions.com with your order number and we'll start the return.

How fast do you ship?

Orders ship in 1-2 business days from our US fulfillment partner. You get a tracking email at the same address you used at checkout as soon as the label is created.

How do I get help?

Email elevere@asiinnovativesolutions.com. Include your order number if you have one. Same-business-day reply, usually faster.
